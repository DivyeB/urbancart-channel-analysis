## Metric definitions (agreed in step 5, 2026-10-25)
Every metric reads the clean tables only (duplicates and test accounts already gone), counts `status = 'delivered'` only, excludes Unknown-channel users with `NOT channel_was_blank` (D11), and covers Jan–Jun 2026.

| # | Metric | Definition | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | New customer | User with ≥1 delivered order, counted once under their acquisition channel (a cancelled first order followed by a delivered one still counts) | 4 paid + Organic |
| M2 | CAC | Channel's total Jan–Jun spend ÷ its new customers (M1) | 4 paid + "All paid" benchmark (= total paid spend ÷ total paid customers, not an average of CACs; candidate kept it) |
| M3 | Revenue per customer | Delivered revenue of the channel's customers ÷ its new customers (not all signups) | 4 paid + Organic |
| M4 | Repeat purchase rate | Customers with ≥2 delivered orders ÷ new customers, as a % | 4 paid + Organic |
| M5 | LTV | M3 × 30% | 4 paid + Organic |
| M6 | LTV:CAC | M5 ÷ M2, 2 decimals | 4 paid |
| M7 | Monthly CAC | Spend in month m ÷ new customers who **signed up** in month m. Jan→Jun change reported in ₹ and %. Zero customers → NULL via `NULLIF`. Plus a sensitivity check on Jan–May cohorts (immature June cohort) | 4 paid × 6 months |
| M8 | Rounding | Calculate from unrounded values; round only for display: money to nearest ₹, % to 1 dp, ratios to 2 dp | — |

Why: M3/M4 use customers because the brief says "÷ number of customers" and LTV:CAC must compare per-customer value with per-customer cost. M7's sensitivity check tests whether a rising trend is real or caused by late cohorts having less time to convert.
