# Reflection

I'm applying for this role, despite the rigour and hard work it demands, because I love problem solving. This case study shows me the kind of challenging problems the role involves. I'm not treating it as a literal example of the day-to-day work, but it captures the mix I enjoy: messy data, careful checking, and a business decision at the end.

I've done this kind of work before. As a Product Analyst at Spinny, I built 50+ SQLMesh models across areas including Marketing, and consolidated 18 Customer Experience ticketing tables into one analytics-ready table adopted by three business teams. That taught me rigour isn't optional: business logic has to be agreed with PMs and engineers, and data validated before anyone relies on it. I find that process rewarding rather than tiring, and I felt the same here, when a single email check turned up test accounts that changed the answer.

I look forward to learning, solving data problems and automating the work with AI. I already use Claude Code for SQL development and testing, and a Data and AI Engineer role is exactly the opportunity to grow that in line with my career goals.

As for why Shiprocket: it's a well-known name in logistics and e-commerce, an area I've been keenly interested in since working in a consumer e-commerce business. This role would let me see a company like that from the inside.
